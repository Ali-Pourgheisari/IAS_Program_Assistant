How do Modules at Informatik work?
Research Groups (Led by Professors)
They offer „Modules“
Can be: 
Courses (lectures + seminars or exercises)
Seminar (3-credit standalone module)
Master Project
Hackathon
Internship (Praktikum)
Theses
All of these courses can be found in the research groups page (https://www.inf.uni-hamburg.de/en/inst/groups.html) or in the STINE Course Catalogue

Independent Study
3 or 6 credits (Either in Focus option or extension option)
Not registered in STiNE. Has a special registariton procedure
Can have up to 6 credits in Focus option. 9 in Total
Can be done in ANY RESEARCH GROUP
 Must be approved by the IAS committee

IAS Course Guidance
05.10.2026
Language classes
Algemeinesprache (Hamburger VHS)
Language Center (Sprachzentrum)
To easily find them, google “DAF A1 A2 Uni Hamburg”
Offers German classes from A1 till A2
Offers 13 other language classes from A1 to B
You get Credits!
Can’t register from STiNE.
You register in their website, do the course and obtain a certificate after passing the course. You send it to the student office to get credits
Check their registration window. Places are limited!
Registration starts from October 9
To easily find them, google “DAF Uni Hamburg”
Offers German classes from B1 till C1
Offers 8 other language classes. 
You get Credits!
You DO register in STiNE.
That means you get your schedule, your notes and everything in STiNE
Registration times are the same as STiNE 
Meaning if you have already prior knowledge and want to start with B1 or higher, You can still register by tomorrow!

If I had known…
You do NOT need to finish exactly in 2 years
If your goal is to stay in Germany, learning German is a MUST so don’t stop even you fulfill the credits. Take more language classes if you can
You can do an internship in companies as a student. It’s worth it! 
Think also about doing a semester abroad. You can and you will get credits
