# Shipping — BrightByte Electronics

BrightByte Electronics is a fictional electronics shop. All information in this document is sample data for learning.

## Delivery area
The shop delivers only to addresses in Germany.

## Processing time
Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days after payment is confirmed. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

## Delivery options
- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days after dispatch. Costs €4.90, or is free for orders with a product subtotal of at least €50.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days after dispatch. Costs €9.90 regardless of order value.

## Tracking
A tracking number is provided when an order is dispatched. A specific order's status, tracking number, and delivery date must be obtained from the order records; this document contains only general shipping rules.

## Delayed or missing deliveries
If the estimated delivery date has passed, contact support with your order ID. If tracking says delivered but the parcel is missing, check with household members and neighbors, then contact support.
