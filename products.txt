# Product Information — BrightByte Electronics

BrightByte Electronics is a fictional electronics shop. All products, prices, and specifications below are sample data for learning. Prices are in euros and include VAT.

## SoundWave H20 Wireless Headphones
- Product ID: P001
- Price: €59.90
- Bluetooth 5.3 wireless connection and optional 3.5 mm wired connection.
- Up to 30 hours of battery life per charge.
- USB-C charging; no active noise cancellation.
- Included accessories: USB-C charging cable, 3.5 mm audio cable, and carrying pouch.

## ClickMini M10 Wireless Mouse
- Product ID: P002
- Price: €24.90
- Connects through the included USB-A wireless receiver; does not support Bluetooth.
- Compatible with Windows 11 and macOS 13 or later.
- Requires one AA battery, included in the box.
- Included accessories: USB-A receiver and one AA battery.

## PowerPort C65 Wall Charger
- Product ID: P003
- Price: €39.90
- One USB-C port with USB Power Delivery up to 65 W.
- Supports input voltage of 100–240 V and has an EU plug.
- Can charge devices that support USB-C Power Delivery; actual charging speed depends on the device and cable.
- Included accessories: one 1-meter USB-C to USB-C cable rated for 100 W.
